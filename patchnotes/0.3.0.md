# Remnants: Ballistics 0.3.0 — First Shots

The first public pre-release of Remnants: Ballistics, a server-authoritative firearms mod for NeoForge 1.21.1.

## Arsenal

- **16 weapons in 7 classes:**
  - **Pistols:** R-9 Sidearm, Ironfang .50 and the Warden .357 revolver.
  - **SMGs:** Viper-9 and Spectre PDW.
  - **Assault rifles:** ARX-16, Raven AK and the Mantis BR bullpup.
  - **Shotguns:** Breacher-12 and Thunderpump.
  - **Snipers:** Ghostline M24 and Longshot .50.
  - **LMGs:** Titan-56 and Bulwark-762.
  - **Experimental:** the Voidcaster and the Eclipse Railgun.
- Semi, burst and full-auto fire modes; tactical and empty reloads; single-round loading; pump and bolt cycling; a charged railgun shot and a void orb projectile.
- 9 ammunition types, crafted at the new **Ammunition Press**.

## Shooting

- Server-validated hitscan with bullet drop, damage falloff and head, body and leg zones.
- Penetration through soft blocks and entities, shallow-angle ricochets, and glass that shatters.
- Aim down sights with exact sight alignment; reflex and holographic reticles; full-screen 4× and 8× scopes.
- Recoil, bloom, sway, and movement, jump and crouch accuracy.
- Tracers, animated muzzle flashes, smoke, ejected brass and shells, impact particles and sounds per material, bullet holes, hit markers, a bullet whiz, and a far-field report for distant gunfire.

## Gunsmithing

- **Gunsmith Workbench** with optic, muzzle, underbarrel, magazine, stock and skin slots, plus a live 3D preview and stat panel.
- 18 attachments with trade-offs and 5 weapon finishes (desert, arctic, crimson, gilded, void).
- Weapon durability, repaired with Weapon Repair Kits.

## Progression

- Steel, brass, polymer, gun parts, precision parts, remnant alloy and void cores.
- Five weapon tiers. Blueprints found in structures unlock tiers 3 to 5 and are never consumed.
- Loot in 25 vanilla chest and mob tables; 12 advancements.

## Multiplayer

- Server-authoritative ammunition, timing, spread, hits and reloads, with client prediction and corrections.
- Lag-compensated player hitboxes and a shared spread seed.
- Replay protection, a jitter-tolerant fire-rate limiter that blocks double taps, aim clamping and a spread floor.
- Duplication-safe workbench and press.

## Configuration

- Client: HUD, crosshair, accessibility, visuals, audio, viewmodel offsets.
- Common: recipe and loot toggles.
- Server: damage and hit zones, handling, ammunition, block interaction, anti-cheat tolerances and per-weapon overrides.

## Downloads

- Minecraft 1.21.1: NeoForge 21.1.264 or newer; Java 21.
- No other mods required. Install on both the server and every client.
